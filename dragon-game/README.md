# Hatchling Hollow

A cozy browser game about raising and painting dragons. Open `index.html` in a browser to play.

- **Hatch**: tap or rub the egg to warm it until it cracks open, then name your dragon.
- **Care**: feed it berries, fish or fire peppers (which make it breathe fire). Rub it to pet it.
- **Paint**: brush, fill, stamp and eraser tools. Paint stays inside the part you start on.
- **Dress**: put on accessories you unlock as your dragon levels up.
- **Grow**: dragons grow from Hatchling (LV 1) to Fledgling (LV 3) to Grown (LV 6), getting horns and back spikes.
- **Lair**: every 4 levels your dragons gain together unlocks another egg, up to 8 dragons.

Progress and paintings are saved in the browser's localStorage.
